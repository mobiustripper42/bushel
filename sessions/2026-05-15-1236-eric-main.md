**Completed:**
- `src/lib/notifications/sms-deep-link.ts`: pure-function `buildSmsUrl({ phone, body })` + `normalizePhone(raw)`. RFC 5724 form `sms:NUMBER?body=...`. - No DB, no UI, no auth, no consumer wiring — that's 4.2's scope. `design/admin-send.{jsx,css}` left untouched.

**Snags worth remembering:**

Resolution was to *pin* the behavior with a test rather than add ext-aware parsing — Annabel doesn't enter extensions, but if she ever does, the test makes the gotcha visible at the point of failure. Lesson: when a lib's V1 behavior is "obviously wrong but won't bite us today," a pinning test beats both adding logic and a comment.
